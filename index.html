[ 01 ] MANIFESTO

To sculpt space is to listen — to context, to climate, to the quiet rituals that occupy a room long after the architect has gone.

An architect & interior designer who is constantly in pursuit of meaningful work — passionate about incorporating balance, harmony and utilitarian thought into the sculpting of an efficient space.

Having practised internationally between London, Chennai and São Paulo, I am adaptive to contextual, cultural and environmentally sensitive needs of a project, and swift to act on the task put forward.

I value being highly organised, pragmatic, ambitious and an uplifting team player.

[ 04 ] PROCESS · TOOLS · METHOD

Method

  1. 01Listen. Site, brief, climate, budget — and the client's quiet hopes.
  2. 02Sketch. Pen, trace, model. Always physical first.
  3. 03Test. Plan, section, façade — iterate hard before committing.
  4. 04Resolve. Detail, material, joint. The space lives in the joint.
  5. 05Deliver. Drawings, models, renders — clear, communicable, calibrated.
